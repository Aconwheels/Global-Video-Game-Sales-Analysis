## Project Overview

This project explores global video game sales to understand how gaming preferences, platforms, publishers and regional markets have influenced the industry's performance over time.

Using Python, I analysed a dataset of 16,598 video game records, focusing on identifying sales trends, comparing market performance and presenting insights through data visualisation.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse global video game sales across different genres and platforms.
- Identify the leading publishers based on total sales.
- Compare gaming preferences across North America, Europe, Japan and other regions.
- Examine annual sales trends and identify peak-performing years.
- Present meaningful findings through Python-based visualisations.

## Dataset

The dataset contains **16,598 records and 11 columns**, including game titles, platforms, release years, genres, publishers and regional sales figures.

**Source:** [Video Game Sales — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/gregorut/videogamesales)

Sales figures are recorded in millions of units.

## Tools and Techniques

- **Python:** Data analysis and processing
- **Pandas:** Data cleaning, filtering, grouping and aggregation
- **NumPy:** Numerical operations
- **Matplotlib:** Data visualisation
- **Jupyter Notebook:** Organising and documenting the analysis

## Key Findings

**1. Action Games Lead Global Sales**

Action was the highest-selling genre, recording approximately 1,751 million units, followed by Sports and Shooter games.

**2. PlayStation 2 Dominated Platform Sales**

The PlayStation 2 recorded approximately 1,256 million units in global sales, making it the leading platform in the dataset.

**3. Nintendo Led Publisher Performance**

Nintendo generated approximately 1,787 million units in recorded global sales, followed by Electronic Arts and Activision.

**4. North America Was the Largest Regional Market**

North America accounted for approximately 4,393 million units, followed by Europe at 2,434 million units and Japan at 1,291 million units.

**5. Sales Peaked in 2008**

The highest recorded annual sales occurred in 2008, reaching approximately 679 million units, with 2009 also showing strong performance.
